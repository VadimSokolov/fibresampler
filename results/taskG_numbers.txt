  Theory note (rem:dichotomy): the argmin ratio scales with mu, so the
  rounded moment-matched Gaussian is NOT a Doeblin teleporter: independence
  proposals teleport only if q dominates pi uniformly on the fibre
  (w* bounded away from 0 in mu).  Tail domination is the missing condition;
  a light-tailed relaxation of a shrinking-mean count target cannot satisfy it.
