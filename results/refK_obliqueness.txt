READING: obliqueness_corr tracks kappa LINEARLY on every enumerable fibre, the
ratio oblq/(sqrt2*kappa) staying near 1 on the band ladder and O(1) on the P1 and
P4 network fibres, with the principal correlation axis aligned to the long fibre
diagonal (align_diag near 1). The covariance spread additionally carries route-
SCALE heterogeneity, which reflection does NOT target (Section 6.1). So the axis
defect kappa IS the obliqueness reflection targets, up to the constant sqrt(2):
the real-data substitution of obliqueness for kappa is justified, not a change of
object.
